Watermark Remover (demo)

Runs fully in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. Paint over the watermark, tap Remove, then Download.

Brush Edge grow tolerance

Limits: it uses a small free AI inpainting model (MI-GAN, MIT licence) that runs on your device. It works well for thin text or logos over smooth or plain areas. Where a watermark covers detailed parts such as faces, text or product details, those pixels are guessed and will not match the original. Use only on images you own or may edit.
